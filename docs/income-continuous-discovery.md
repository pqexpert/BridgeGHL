# Standing Income source discovery

The existing hourly operational caller runs a bounded public employer intake
before the existing Income research controller. Empty imported queues now lead
to discovery; an empty source response remains a quiet, observable no-change.
This covers Income research on two reviewed employer boards, not the whole job
market or all RSC work.

Authority is the owner/version-checked RBO-97:12221 intake delegation, followed
by RBO-97:12130 for source-locked research. Eligible postings explicitly state
US country, remote work and contractor/freelance/part-time commitment, with a
support, help-desk, rater, evaluator, annotation or AI-training title. Candidate
fit, hours, eligibility and work portability remain unknown.

One page of 25 per poll, at most one poll/hour, 24 public HTTP reservations/day,
six new record reservations/day, one intake/invocation. Failures and uncertain
writes consume reservations. Retry-After, six-hour recovery backoff and source
identity/authentication quarantine are durable. Cursor and bounded public-ID
buffers live in the existing controller ledger. No extra scheduler, database,
Actor, Wazuh collector or credentials route is installed.

Historical public role identities, current internal-context CRM notes, current
queue identities and completed model units prevent duplicate intake. Only the
existing internal Income context may receive an unqualified source-keyed
opportunity and one source note. Preview, exact authority CAS, journaled execute,
native source readback and Jira receipt precede queue admission. A frozen effect
plan survives uncertainty; no application, person/contact creation, outreach,
qualification or stage promotion is authorized by this producer.

The existing one-at-a-time Duck model path retains its 24 daily reservations and
no paid fallback. Offline or paused local inference defers intake. Existing work
is processed first. Source discovery and research share the existing controller
and Income locks, and remain distinct from the broader hosted Actor mission.

Operational metadata: `/var/lib/ziji-fabric/dispatch/continuation/state.json`
under `source_discovery`; caller metadata: adjacent `caller-latest.json` under
`source_discovery`; provider intent/result metadata: existing Bridge audit log.
All are on the VPS; private queue/ledger contents must not be committed or copied
into public reports. CLI defaults to no-provider preview:
`python3 scripts/income_source_discovery.py`. Execute only through the existing
protected operational runner and source-owned cadence.

Lever listing/filter/country semantics: https://github.com/lever/postings-api.
